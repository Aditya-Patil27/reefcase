  • Idea 1Phone video of a school physics experiment → SAM 3.1 tracks the moving object → fitted physical law with error bars (a pendulum gives g, an incline gives friction). Any phone becomes a lab instrument.
  • Idea 55Agent that designs the next experiment: after measuring, Muse Spark proposes the next parameter value that best separates two competing models.
